# HITAM Hostels — Comprehensive Architectural & Environmental Photography Guide

This photography reference brief details all requested shots across the **3 campus hostels** at Hyderabad Institute of Technology and Management (HITAM). 

Use this checklist to plan sessions during optimal daylight (morning soft light 7:30 AM – 10:30 AM and golden hour 4:30 PM – 6:15 PM) and twilight/evening for illuminated campus ambiance.

---

## Technical Standards & Visual Guidelines
- **Orientation:**
  - **Hero & Web Banners:** Wide landscape 16:9 (Minimum resolution: 3840 × 2160 @ 300 DPI).
  - **Facility Cards & Mobile App:** 4:3 and 1:1 square detail shots.
  - **Vertical Feature Stories:** 9:16 portrait format for campus notices/mobile banners.
- **Color & Style Tone:** Warm natural sunlight, crisp forest greens, high dynamic range (HDR bracketed exposures for interiors so windows aren't blown out).
- **Staging:** Rooms and facilities must be tidy, beds neatly made, clutter removed, natural light maximized with curtains drawn back.
- **People / Human Element:** Candid shots of students studying, collaborating in common rooms, or walking in green landscaped corridors (candid, authentic, non-staged feel).
