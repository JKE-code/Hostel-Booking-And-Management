ADMIN ROLES & PERMISSIONS:
================================================================================

Super Admin:
  - Access to all hostels
  - Create/manage admin accounts
  - View all reports
  - Configure system settings

Hostel Admin:
  - Manage their assigned hostel
  - Student allocation
  - Fee management
  - Complaint management

Block Warden:
  - Manage their assigned block
  - Student allocation for their block
  - Leave approvals
  - Complaint tracking

Staff:
  - Support personnel
  - Handle complaints
  - Maintenance tasks
  - Limited access

Student:
  - Read-only access
  - View room info
  - Pay fees online
  - Apply leave
  - Submit complaints
